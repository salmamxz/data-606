# Agile + Scrum Research

## What is Agile?

Agile is a way of working where a project is developed in smaller parts rather than trying to build everything at once.

The team works in short cycles, gets feedback regularly, and makes changes when needed. This is useful because requirements can change during a project, and it allows the team to deliver useful features earlier.

The main ideas behind Agile include:

- Working closely as a team
- Communicating regularly
- Getting feedback from customers
- Delivering working software frequently
- Being able to respond to changes
- Continuously improving the way the team works

## What is Scrum?

Scrum is a framework used to help teams work in an Agile way.

The work is divided into smaller pieces and completed in short periods called **Sprints**. A Sprint usually lasts between 1 and 4 weeks.

At the end of a Sprint, the team should have a working part of the product that can be reviewed and improved.

### Scrum Roles

There are three main roles/accountabilities in Scrum:

- **Product Owner** – decides what work is most important and manages the Product Backlog.
- **Scrum Master** – helps the team follow Scrum, supports the team, and helps remove obstacles.
- **Developers** – design, build and test the product.

### Scrum Events

Some of the main Scrum events are:

- **Sprint Planning** – the team decides what work they will complete during the Sprint.
- **Daily Scrum** – a short daily meeting where the team checks progress and discusses any issues.
- **Sprint Review** – the team demonstrates what they have completed and gets feedback.
- **Sprint Retrospective** – the team discusses what went well, what did not go well, and what they can improve.

## Example: CRM Project

For a CRM system, instead of trying to build the whole system before showing it to the customer, an Agile/Scrum team could develop it in stages.

### Sprint 1
- Create customer registration
- Create customer login
- Set up the basic database

### Sprint 2
- Create customer profiles
- Allow staff to view and update customer information

### Sprint 3
- Add booking management
- Allow staff to create and update bookings

### Sprint 4
- Add automatic reminders
- Send reminders to customers about upcoming bookings

### Sprint 5
- Test the system
- Fix issues
- Improve features based on customer feedback

This approach means the customer can see progress throughout the project. If their requirements change, the team can adapt the backlog and work on the new requirements in a future Sprint.

## Agile vs Scrum

| Agile | Scrum |
|---|---|
| A way of thinking and working | A framework for applying Agile |
| Focuses on flexibility and responding to change | Uses specific roles, events and artefacts |
| Can be used in different ways | Has defined Scrum practices |
| Focuses on delivering value and feedback | Uses Sprints to organise the work |

### In simple terms

**Agile is the overall approach, while Scrum is one framework that helps a team work in an Agile way.**

The main idea is to **build in small steps, get feedback, learn from it, and keep improving the product.**
