# Generative AI and LLM Research

## What is Generative AI?

Generative AI can create new content such as text, images, code, audio and video. Tools like ChatGPT use patterns learned from large amounts of data to generate responses.

## Common Uses

* Writing and summarising
* Coding and debugging
* Customer service
* Marketing
* Research and brainstorming
* Data analysis
* Creating images and media

## What are LLMs?

LLM stands for **Large Language Model**. LLMs are trained on large amounts of text and learn patterns in language. They can generate text, answer questions, summarise information and write code.

Examples include **GPT, Claude, Gemini and Llama**.

## How I Use AI

I use AI for:

* Understanding unfamiliar topics
* Python and coding support
* Improving emails and messages
* Brainstorming
* Planning and organising tasks
* Research

I use AI to work more efficiently, but I still check the information it provides.

## Four Properties of AI

* **Next-token prediction:** Predicting what comes next.
* **Knowledge:** Using information learned or provided.
* **Working memory:** Using information within the current context.
* **Steerability:** Following instructions to influence the output.

## AI and CRMs

AI is increasingly being used in CRM systems for:

* AI assistants
* Customer service automation
* Summaries
* Predictive insights
* AI agents

A good prompt should include clear context, a specific task, timeframe and expected format.

## Prompting Strategies

* Be specific
* Give context
* Assign a role
* Specify the format
* Provide examples

Example:

> "Explain SQL joins to a beginner using a simple real-world example. Keep it under 200 words."

## The 4Ds of AI

* **Delegation:** Decide what AI should do.
* **Description:** Clearly explain what you need.
* **Discernment:** Check the quality and accuracy of the output.
* **Diligence:** Use AI responsibly and consider privacy and bias.

## Current AI Landscape

Major LLM platforms include:

* OpenAI: GPT
* Anthropic: Claude
* Google: Gemini
* Meta: Llama
* Mistral: Mistral models

AI is also increasingly being used with **Python, SQL, data visualisation, vector databases and RAG**.

## Main Takeaway

AI is becoming part of many different jobs. For someone working in data, it is important to know how to use AI effectively while still checking its output and using your own judgement.
