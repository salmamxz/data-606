# NHS Test and Trace

## What were the goals?

The main goals were to:
- Test people for COVID-19.
- Trace people who had been in contact with someone who tested positive.
- Help people isolate to reduce the spread.
- Provide data and information to help the government make decisions. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Why was it considered a failure?

The programme struggled to reach people quickly enough and did not consistently meet its targets. There were also problems with how the programme was organised, its reliance on contractors and the high cost.

It also failed to prevent further lockdowns, which was one of the expectations placed on the programme. 

## Who was running it?

## What was the impact?

The main impact was that COVID-19 continued to spread and the programme did not have the expected effect on reducing transmission. There were also concerns about the large amount of public money spent, with a £22 billion budget for 2020–21 and £13.5 billion actually spent. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

### My takeaway

For me, the main lesson is that having a large amount of data and resources isn't enough. The system also needs good data quality, fast processes, clear management and effective communication.
